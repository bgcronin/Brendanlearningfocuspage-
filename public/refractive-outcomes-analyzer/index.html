Lens Constant Optimiser
Personalise IOL formula constants to eliminate systematic refractive bias in your surgical cohort.

Step 1 — Enter Nominal Constants

Enter the manufacturer's nominal constant for each IOL model and formula combination detected in your dataset. Values are pre-filled from published data where available — always verify against IOLCon (iolcon.org) or the manufacturer before optimising. Note: the Kane and EVO calculators take the manufacturer's SRK/T A-constant as their nominal input constant, so those columns are pre-filled with the SRK/T value.
⚠ Clinical Disclaimer: These optimised constants are derived from this surgeon's dataset and should be validated prospectively before routine clinical use. Cross-reference with IOLCon (iolcon.org) published values.
A minimum of 50 eyes per IOL model per formula is a widely used minimum for constant optimisation; 100 or more eyes further improves reliability (Aristodemou et al., JCRS 2011).
Method: optimised constant = nominal constant + mean prediction error × formula-specific factor — a linear approximation of full iterative optimisation. The 95% confidence interval uses the t-distribution. Projected "after" percentages are in-sample estimates.
