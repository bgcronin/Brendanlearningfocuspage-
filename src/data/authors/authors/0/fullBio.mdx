Dr Cronin has developed a reputation for managing **technically demanding cataract cases**, including:

* Cataract surgery after LASIK, PRK and other refractive procedures
* Irregular corneas and keratoconus
* High astigmatism requiring toric and advanced lens planning

These cases require careful planning, advanced diagnostics, and a deep understanding of corneal optics — an area in which Dr Cronin has subspecialty expertise.

## Precision refractive outcomes

For patients seeking independence from glasses, cataract surgery is no longer just about removing the cataract — it is a refractive procedure.

Dr Cronin has a strong focus on **refractive cataract surgery**, using modern intraocular lens technology and detailed pre-operative analysis to optimise visual outcomes.

This includes the use of toric, extended depth of focus (EDOF), and multifocal lenses where appropriate, with a tailored approach based on each patient's eye and visual goals.

This is why Dr Cronin offers Ray-Tracing LASIK at Focus Vision – he demands the best technology and the best refractive outcomes for his patients.

## Training, research and leadership

Dr Cronin trained in Brisbane and completed advanced fellowship training in Corneal, Refractive and Anterior Segment Surgery at the internationally recognised Manchester Royal Eye Hospital in the United Kingdom.

He is Director of Education at the Queensland Eye Institute and is actively involved in teaching and training ophthalmologists.

He has participated in over 20 international clinical trials and holds an international patent in corneal cross-linking technology. His work reflects a continued commitment to advancing surgical techniques and improving patient outcomes.
