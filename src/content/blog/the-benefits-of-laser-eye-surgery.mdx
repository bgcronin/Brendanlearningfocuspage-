Imagine a life without glasses or contact lenses. For many who rely on these visual aids, it may seem like an unattainable dream. After all, they have become accustomed to the inconvenience of blurry vision and the constant need for correction. [Laser eye surgery](/vision-correction/laser-eye-surgery) has emerged as a game-changer in the field of vision correction. By reshaping the cornea using advanced laser technology, this procedure can effectively correct nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism, offering a life-changing opportunity to see the world with newfound clarity, and the benefits of laser eye surgery extend beyond the realm of easy living.

## **Convenience and Clear Vision**

The primary and most noticeable benefit of laser eye surgery for vision correction is the improvement in eyesight, leading to the elimination of the [need to wear glasses](/vision-correction/laser-for-reading-glasses). Laser eye surgery eliminates the hassle of constantly having to deal with glasses or contact lenses. You no longer need to worry about cleaning your glasses, carrying contact lens solution, or replacing lost or damaged eyewear. By undergoing laser eye surgery, you can enjoy the convenience of waking up each morning with clear vision and the freedom to engage in various activities without the restriction of visual aids.

## **Reduced Risk of Eye Infections**

Wearing contact lenses comes with a heightened risk of eye infections, especially when they are not maintained properly. [Laser eye surgery](/vision-correction/laser-eye-surgery) eliminates the reliance on contacts altogether, effectively lowering the risk of eye infections and related complications. By opting for laser eye surgery, individuals can significantly promote better eye health, reducing the necessity for antibiotic treatments that may arise from contact lens-related issues.

## **Decreased Risk of Cataracts**

[Cataracts](/vision-correction/cataract-surgery) are a common eye condition that affects millions of people worldwide, particularly as they age. The condition occurs when the natural lens in the eye becomes cloudy, leading to progressive vision loss that can affect everyday activities such as reading, driving, and even recognising faces.

While cataracts are typically treated through surgery, new research suggests that laser eye surgery may be able to reduce the risk of developing cataracts in the first place. Laser eye surgery may reduce the risk of cataract development by up to 50%\*. The exact mechanism behind this observed reduction in cataract risk is not fully understood, it is suggested the removal of the corneal tissue during laser eye surgery may have a protective effect on the lens, potentially limiting the accumulation of cataract-forming proteins.
