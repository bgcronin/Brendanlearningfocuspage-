Focus Vision is part of an international network of vision correction clinics that pride themselves on exceptional patient care, experienced surgeons and cutting edge technology. One of our partner clinics is [Vision Care Clinic](https://visioncareclinic.com) in Bristol in the UK. Dr Gunn and Dr Cronin regularly meet with Mr Kieren Darcy at international conferences to ensure their clinics remain at the cutting edge of technology for all types of laser eye surgery, lens replacement surgery and cataract surgery. Both clinics are regularly involved in pivotal clinical trials of groundbreaking new technologies.

Maintaining these international connections ensures that the patients at both clinics receive the highest standards of care possible with the best trained surgeons, the most pleasant and efficient clinics, the best surgical experience and most importantly - the best vision!
