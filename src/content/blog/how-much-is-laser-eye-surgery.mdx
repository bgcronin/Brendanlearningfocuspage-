At [Focus Vision](/about-focus-vision), we understand that the decision to undergo [laser eye surgery](/vision-correction/laser-eye-surgery) is significant, not only for your vision but also for your finances. The cost of laser eye surgery can vary depending on several factors, including the specific procedure, technology used, and individual patient needs. Our team is committed to providing transparent [pricing](/costs) and comprehensive consultations to help you make an informed decision. So, how much is laser eye surgery at Focus Vision?

![A man in a suit smiles while sitting at a desk in a modern office, gesturing with one hand and holding a tablet, ready to discuss your free assessment for LASIK surgery.](../../assets/images/blog/how-much-is-laser-eye-surgery/img-EyeSurgery.jpg)

## **All-Laser Femto LASIK**

For LASIK surgery at Focus Vision, the cost is $4,250 per eye. We also offer a flexible payment option, allowing you to finance your procedure for just $41 a week over a 24-month interest-free plan.

The price of $4,250 per eye for LASIK surgery at Focus Vision includes several valuable benefits to ensure a smooth and successful experience. If you qualify, you'll receive our advanced Ray tracing treatments as part of the package. Additionally, the cost covers all pre-operative assessments and tests, as well as comprehensive aftercare and postoperative appointments for one year.

## **PRK and Trans PRK**

PRK and Trans PRK costs $3,500 per eye. We offer a payment plan of $34 a week over a 24-month, interest-free period. It is essential to offer flexible payment plans for our customers, enabling them to achieve the clearer vision they desire without financial strain.

The price for our [laser eye surgery](/vision-correction/laser-eye-surgery) includes No Touch Transepithelial PRK (TransPRK), a state-of-the-art procedure that reshapes the cornea without the need for a blade or flap. The price also includes comprehensive benefits such as all pre-operative tests and assessments, as well as one year of aftercare and postoperative appointments.

## **Lenticule Extraction**

[Lenticule Extraction](/vision-correction/laser-eye-surgery) costs $4,250 per eye which can be paid outright. Alternatively, there is a 24-month interest-free payment plan offering our patients to pay $41 per week.

The price includes the latest Flapless Lenticule surgery, which refers to a technique that is designed to correct [refractive vision errors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refractive_error) without the need for creating a corneal flap. Again, the cost includes all pre-operative assessments and tests and all postoperative appointments for a year.

## **Implantable Contact Lens (ICL)**

The [ICL procedure](/vision-correction/implantable-contact-lens) costs $7,000 per eye, or $68 a week with our 24-month interest-free payment plan. Like all surgeries, the price includes pre-operative assessments and all aftercare appointments for a year. All hospital and anaesthetic fees are included in this price. Customised lenses are also included without additional fees, ensuring each patient receives the most suitable and effective solution for their unique vision needs.

## **Refractive Lens Exchange (RLE)**

[RLE surgery](/vision-correction/refractive-lens-exchange) at Focus Vision costs $6,000 per eye. Like all our surgeries we provide, our patients can spread the cost over a 24-month interest-free payment plan, paying $58 a week. The price includes all pre-operative assessments, all hospital and anaesthetic fees, all premium lens options without additional fees, and all aftercare appointments for a year.

Including postoperative care in our pricing ensures that our clients receive the best possible care and experience with Focus Vision.
