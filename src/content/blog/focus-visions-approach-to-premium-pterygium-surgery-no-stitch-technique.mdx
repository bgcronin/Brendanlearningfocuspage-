## Focus Vision's Approach to Premium Pterygium Surgery: No-Stitch Technique

Pterygium, commonly known as "surfer's eye," is a common condition, especially here in sunny Queensland. This raised, wedge-shaped growth on the conjunctiva (the clear membrane covering the white part of your eye) can cause irritation, redness, and even affect your vision if it grows onto the cornea. While not usually a dangerous condition, a pterygium can be uncomfortable and cosmetically concerning.

### What is a Pterygium and Why Might Surgery Be Needed?

A pterygium is thought to be caused by prolonged exposure to ultraviolet (UV) light, wind, and dust. Symptoms can include:

- A persistent feeling of something in your eye
- Redness and inflammation
- Dryness or itchiness
- Blurred vision (if the pterygium grows over the cornea)
- Cosmetic concerns about the eye's appearance

While eye drops and ointments can manage symptoms, surgery is the only way to remove a pterygium. Surgery is typically recommended if the pterygium is causing significant discomfort, affecting your vision, growing rapidly, or if you're concerned about its appearance.

### The Focus Vision Difference: No-Stitch Premium Pterygium Surgery

Traditionally, pterygium surgery involved removing the growth and then stitching a graft of healthy conjunctival tissue (an autograft) into its place to help prevent recurrence. While effective, stitches on the eye can be a source of irritation and prolonged discomfort during the healing process.

At Focus Vision, our **premium no-stitch technique** revolutionises this aspect. Instead of sutures, we use a special surgical adhesive (fibrin glue) to secure the conjunctival autograft. This offers several key advantages for our Brisbane patients:

- **Significantly Reduced Discomfort:** Avoiding stitches means less irritation, grittiness, and pain after the procedure.
- **Faster Healing and Recovery:** Patients typically experience a quicker and more comfortable recovery period.
- **Improved Cosmetic Outcomes:** The no-stitch technique often leads to a smoother, more natural appearance of the eye once healed.
- **Low Recurrence Rates:** When combined with a meticulous surgical technique and the placement of an autograft, this advanced approach helps to minimise the chances of the pterygium growing back. At Focus Vision, we are proud of our very low recurrence rates (around 1 in 1000).
