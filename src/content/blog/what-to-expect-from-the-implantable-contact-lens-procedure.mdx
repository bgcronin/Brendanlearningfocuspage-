Implantable Contact Lenses (ICLs) are an innovative solution for those seeking to improve their vision without the need for glasses or traditional contact lenses. At [Focus Vision in Brisbane](/home), the ICL procedure has transformed the way many individuals with refractive vision problems approach eye care. If you're considering ICLs as an alternative to laser surgery or glasses, it's essential to understand what to expect from the implantable contact lens procedure.

## **Initial Consultation and Assessment**

The journey to [implantable contact lenses](/vision-correction/implantable-contact-lens) begins with a detailed consultation at Focus Vision. During this initial appointment, your eye health and vision will be thoroughly evaluated. The specialist will conduct several tests to measure the shape and size of your eyes, check for any underlying conditions, and ensure that you’re a good candidate for the procedure.

ICLs are particularly effective for individuals with moderate to severe nearsightedness, farsightedness, or astigmatism, who may not be suitable candidates for laser vision correction (like [LASIK](/blog?tag=LASIK)). Your doctor will discuss your options, including whether ICLs are the best choice for your specific needs.

## **The ICL Procedure**

Once you've been deemed a suitable candidate, it’s time to prepare for the procedure. The ICL surgery is typically performed in a sterile, comfortable environment, and the entire process usually takes about 15-30 minutes per eye. At Focus Vision in Brisbane, patients can expect the latest technology and high levels of care.

On the day of the surgery, you’ll be given a mild sedative to help you relax. Local anaesthesia will be administered to numb the eyes, ensuring that you remain comfortable and pain-free throughout the procedure. Unlike laser vision correction, the ICL does not require the cornea to be reshaped, which can make it a preferable option for certain patients.

The surgeon will create a small incision in the cornea to insert the ICL, which is a thin, flexible lens that will sit comfortably inside the eye, between the cornea and the natural lens. The lens is designed to correct refractive errors without the need for an external lens. After the lens is positioned correctly, the incision will heal naturally, and no stitches are required.

## **ICL Recovery and Aftercare**

One of the key advantages of the ICL procedure is its quick recovery time. Many patients report an improvement in their vision almost immediately after the procedure. However, it’s essential to follow the aftercare instructions provided by the team at Focus Vision to ensure a smooth recovery process.

In the first few days after the surgery, you may experience mild discomfort, including dryness, light sensitivity, or slight blurriness. This is normal and should subside within a few days. You will also be prescribed eye drops to reduce the risk of infection and promote healing.

Most patients are able to return to normal activities within a few days, although it’s important to avoid activities like swimming, heavy exercise, or anything that could put strain on the eyes for about a week. Follow-up appointments will be scheduled to ensure your eyes are healing correctly, and any adjustments or further treatments will be discussed.

## **Long-Term Results with Implantable Contact Lenses**

The results of implantable contact lenses are often long-lasting, providing sharp, clear vision without the need for glasses or daily contact lens maintenance. While the lenses are designed to last, it’s important to have regular eye exams to monitor the health of your eyes and ensure the lenses remain properly positioned.
