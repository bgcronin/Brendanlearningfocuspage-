Dr Brendan Cronin is a corneal, cataract and refractive surgeon and the
co-founder of Focus Vision in Brisbane, where he practises alongside Dr David
Gunn. After graduating from the University of Queensland, he completed
subspecialty corneal fellowship training at the Manchester Royal Eye Hospital
in the UK before returning to Queensland.

Dr Cronin is the Director of Education at the Queensland Eye Institute and
teaches corneal, cataract and refractive surgical techniques to
ophthalmologists around the world. His special interests include keratoconus
— including corneal cross-linking and CAIRS — laser vision correction, and
premium cataract and pterygium surgery. He co-created the CAIRSPlan surgical
planning tool and the free Focus Vision Refractive Outcomes Analyzer used by
surgeons internationally.
